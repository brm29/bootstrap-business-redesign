About Us

Why Choose Us

At AllyCats Hair Studio, we believe healthy hair is beautiful hair. We are committed to providing exceptional hair care in a warm, welcoming environment where every client feels valued. With a passion for quality, affordability, and personalised service, we take the time to understand your unique hair needs and help you achieve your hair goals with confidence.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower every client to look and feel their best by delivering professional hair care tailored to their individual needs. We are dedicated to promoting healthy, beautiful hair through expert advice, high-quality services, and a genuine commitment to customer satisfaction. Every visit is an opportunity to leave feeling refreshed, confident, and revitalised.

What We Do

Founded by Alicia Rorke, AllyCats Hair Studio offers personalised hair care tailored to every client. From precision cuts and colour services to nourishing treatments and expert advice, we focus on creating healthy, beautiful hair. Using quality products and professional techniques, we help you achieve a look you'll love with confidence.
